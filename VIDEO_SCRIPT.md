# Trace Video Script

Target duration: approximately 1 minute 50 seconds.

## 0:00–0:12 — Problem introduction

**Saaheer, on camera:**

> Hi, I’m Saaheer. Imagine a customer’s washing machine suddenly stops draining. The customer does not know what went wrong or what information the technician will need. Instead of repeatedly explaining the problem to support teams, the customer opens Trace.

## 0:12–0:35 — Customer experience

Enter the following serial number:

```text
DEMO-WASHER-01
```

**Saaheer:**

> The customer enters the serial number, and the agent identifies the exact product. Based on the product—whether it is a washing machine, microwave, or another appliance—the agent prepares a relevant set of questions to understand the problem.

When prompted, answer:

> The washing machine is not draining. There is water inside the drum, and it makes a humming sound.

Then answer:

> Yes, it shows error code E21.

Upload the sample photograph.

## 0:35–0:52 — Agent takes action

Submit the service request.

**Saaheer:**

> The agent reviews the customer’s answers, prepares technician instructions, creates a real Freshdesk ticket, automatically assigns the relevant technician, and sends the service details to the customer through WhatsApp.

Show the ticket, assignment, notification, and new-job badge.

> The complete customer context is handed over without the customer having to repeat the problem.

## 0:52–1:00 — Technician introduction

**Induj, on camera:**

> Hi, I’m Induj. Now let’s look at the technician’s experience.

Open the Technician tab.

## 1:00–1:28 — Technician experience

**Induj:**

> Before beginning the visit, the technician can see the identified product, reported symptoms, customer evidence, and the inspection plan prepared by the agent.

Select the following actions:

1. **Start job**
2. **Power disconnected**
3. **Blockage found**

Pause on the replanning animation.

**Induj:**

> This is not a static checklist. Based on the technician’s findings, the agent adapts the inspection plan in real time. When the technician confirms a filter blockage, the agent removes the unnecessary hose and pump checks and recommends cleaning the filter followed by a drain-cycle test.

Continue with:

4. **Filter cleared**
5. **Drain cycle passed**
6. **Complete job**
7. Confirm **Blocked drain filter**

## 1:28–1:42 — Resolution

**Induj:**

> Once the technician confirms the actual issue, the agent records the result, updates Freshdesk, and automatically closes the ticket.

Switch back to the Customer tab.

> The customer can now see the confirmed finding and completed service status.

## 1:42–1:52 — Closing

**Saaheer:**

> Our agent manages the complete service journey—from the customer’s first conversation to confirmed resolution.

**Induj:**

> It gives technicians the right context before they arrive and provides customers with a faster, more connected service experience.

**Both:**

> This is Trace.
