# Trace Demo Positioning

Trace is an AI-powered after-sales service agent.

Based on the identified product—whether it is a washing machine, microwave, or another appliance—Trace prepares a relevant set of questions to understand the problem and collect supporting evidence.

Trace then creates a Freshdesk ticket, automatically assigns the relevant technician, and notifies the customer through WhatsApp.

It also prepares a tailored list of inspection questions and tasks for the technician. As the technician reports findings, Trace adapts the remaining tasks and recommends the next best action.

Once the technician confirms the issue, Trace records the result and automatically closes the Freshdesk ticket, managing the complete service journey from customer report to resolution.

## Closing line

Trace gives technicians the right context before they arrive and guides every service request from the first customer conversation to confirmed resolution.
